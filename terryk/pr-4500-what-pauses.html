Four pages on this PR. This one: which parts of training keep running during a snapshot.

Background. A snapshot runs in its own task, _rollout_checkpoint_pump, not in the train pump or the rollout pump. Nothing tells the other pumps to stop. They wait only when they reach something the snapshot holds. The snapshot holds three things: the dispatch gate, Gym itself (parked), and the TQ barrier. Dotted words have a short explanation: hover or tap them.

Ten parts, three phases

Each row name links to the code that decides that row. On main, the middle column already happened at every rollout snapshot; the left column is what turn adds.

The three places the trainer actually waits

  • It runs out of data. No new rollouts finish while Gym is parked. If the buffer empties during the Gym phase, the trainer has nothing to train on.
  • Every TQ write during the TQ save. Claiming a batch (claim_for_training), the advantage writeback (:5873) and end-of-step cleanup (:3503) are all TQ writes. The GPU math in between keeps going. Refit comes after the cleanup (:3670), so it runs late too.
  • A trainer checkpoint waits for the whole snapshot. It takes the same lock (:5300 vs :4823), held from the first Gym step until the snapshot folder is renamed. If refit was put off for that save (:3713-3728), the generation engines stay asleep for that time too. The lock was shared on main; the Gym phase makes the wait longer. review comment — single_controller.py:4727

It goes the other way too: the snapshot gives way to the trainer. It is skipped while an optimizer step is being committed, and thrown away if a train step finishes during the Gym phase. review comment — single_controller.py:4906

So what. A turn snapshot stops rollout production, not training. Training slows only when it runs out of buffered data, hits a TQ write during the TQ save, or tries to save a trainer checkpoint. The timers that watch single rollouts are paused. The stall watchdog is not, but any finished train step resets it, and by default it only warns. review comment — config.py:719
