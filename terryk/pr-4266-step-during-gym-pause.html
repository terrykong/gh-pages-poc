A training step that finishes during the Gym pause throws the snapshot away

Explainer for NVIDIA-NeMo/RL #4266. Permalinks pin the PR head 4db8f66; "before" links pin the base ed14402.

Before
A periodic rollout snapshot took the data-plane barrier and checked that no training step had finished since it started. If one had, it dropped the attempt. That cost almost nothing, because nothing had been paused yet.
This PR
With Gym checkpointing on, each attempt first pauses Gym and waits for it to drain and write its state, and only then runs the same check. A step that finishes during that wait still drops the attempt, but now after rollouts were stopped for the whole pause.
Status
Open — not fixed in this PR. No data is lost; the cost is wasted pauses and snapshots that never land. Raised in the review; a fix is proposed below.

Ten pages on this stack — this one: #4266: a training step that lands while Gym is paused.

Background. Single Controller trains and generates at the same time. Every few minutes it tries a rollout snapshot. The snapshot is filed under the newest trainer checkpoint, say step_10, and must describe the rollout data exactly as it was when those weights were saved. With this PR, Gym's paused agents are saved in the same snapshot, so Gym has to stop first. Dotted words have a short explanation: hover or tap them.

An attempt records the current step (10) and prepares a folder under step_10 (single_controller.py:4626). It then closes rollout admission and asks Gym to drain and write its state (:4648). The training loop is not paused, so it keeps going. Only after Gym is done does the attempt take the data-plane barrier and compare the step again (:4664). If step 11 finished in between, the attempt deletes the folder, aborts Gym, and logs skipped (:4911). A skipped attempt never counts as a failure.

Which snapshot. This is the periodic snapshot, started every snapshot_attempt_interval_s by its own loop, which runs beside training (:801-811). The snapshot taken at each trainer checkpoint runs inside the training loop, so training can't move during it, and it can't hit this race. A periodic attempt only gets as far as pausing Gym while the step right after a trainer checkpoint is training; at other times it stops early, before touching Gym (:4598).

One attempt, second by second

Why it can't just finish into step_10 anyway. When step 11 commits, it deletes the rollout groups it trained on (_cleanup_consumed_metas_unlocked). A step_10 snapshot taken after that is missing those groups. But the step_10 weights never learned from them, so a restart from it would silently drop them. So the check is right to throw the attempt away. The problem is that the throw-away now happens after a 70 s Gym pause, not before one.

How often. An attempt that paused Gym is wasted whenever a step finishes during the pause. In this example the pause (70 s) is longer than a step (40 s), so every attempt overlaps the end of some step, as long as the buffer holds enough finished rollouts to keep training going while new rollouts are stopped. The check before the pause only looks at whether a trainer checkpoint exists for the current step (:4598), not at whether a step is about to finish.

What would fix it — proposed, not in the PR

Nothing below is implemented or run. It is one way to do it, written against the PR head.

Where the fix lands: RL only, one file. single_controller.py, about 30 added lines and 7 changed. No Gym change: when Gym's prepare call is cancelled, it already resumes every agent it paused (nemo_gym.py:1261), and Ray delivers that cancel to an async actor as a normal asyncio cancel (Ray 2.56.1 worker.py:3321).

The idea: the snapshot can't land once a step commits, so stop waiting for Gym the moment that happens. Training tells the snapshot "a step is committing". The snapshot cancels the Gym prepare right away, and Gym resumes its agents. In the example, rollouts restart at 40 s instead of 95 s. The snapshot is still lost, but training never waits.

What each piece does, in order: training sets the event where it starts a commit (PPO :3276, GRPO :3404). The waiting prepare sees it, cancels, and waits for Gym to finish resuming. The existing error path in _prepare_and_commit_gym_checkpoint then sends the same-ID abort. That abort does nothing, because Gym already cleared the checkpoint, and it reopens rollout admission. The attempt ends as skipped, exactly as today, just 55 s sooner.

With save_period: 1. Every step is a trainer checkpoint, and each one takes the same save lock as the periodic snapshot (:5101). Today, step 11's checkpoint waits for the doomed periodic attempt to finish its whole Gym pause, then runs its own Gym pause, so training sits through both. With the change above, the periodic attempt gives up as soon as step 11 commits. Step 11's checkpoint then takes the Gym snapshot itself, at the newer step, so nothing is lost.

So what. Nothing is lost or corrupted: throwing the attempt away is the correct response. But while a step is shorter than Gym's drain plus write, periodic Gym snapshots between trainer checkpoints can nearly all be wasted. Each one stops new rollouts for the full pause, and each is logged only as skipped with reason trainer_state_changed. With save_period: 1, the wasted pause also holds up the next trainer checkpoint, so training waits too.
