Six pages on #4818, one question each. This one asks what the other score-centering PR covers that #4818 does not.

Background. Score centering needs, for every generated token, the K tokens the sampler thought most likely and their probabilities. Call these "the K candidates". The trainer then subtracts the score the sampler expected from the score it actually got. Both PRs record the K candidates in vLLM and compute the same correction in the loss. They differ in which trainer they support, how the candidates travel from vLLM to the trainer, and how the correction joins the loss. v1 and v2 are the two GRPO trainers. Dotted words have a short explanation: hover or tap them.

Where each PR moves the K candidates

Example: a 4-token prompt and 3 generated tokens, so a row is S = 7 tokens. K = 2. At the first generated token the sampler's top 2 are ids 17 and 4, with q = 0.45 and 0.25 (log q = −0.80, −1.39). The trainer gives them p = 0.50 and 0.32. [3, 2] means [generated tokens, K candidates], and [7, 2] means [row tokens, K candidates].

The two PRs give the same numbers at the start and at the end. Both record ids 17 and 4, both read p = 0.50 and 0.32, and both compute c = q − ρ·p = −0.383 and −0.283, where ρ = (1 − 0.70) ÷ (1 − 0.82) = 1.667 scales the part of the vocabulary nobody recorded. They differ in the middle (how the K candidates reach the trainer) and at the end (what the correction is added to).

The math is the same estimator. Both compute c = m − α·p on the K candidates, with the same IS, TIS and ICE-POP rules for m and α (#4818 score_centering.py L89, #4833 clipped_pg.py L99). The only difference is what that term is added to. A is the advantage, y the sampled token, w its importance weight, sg means no gradient:

\mathcal{L}_{\#4818} = \htmlClass{t1}{\underbrace{\mathcal{L}_{\text{policy gradient}}}_{\text{any configured PG loss}}} + A\sum_{v\in\text{K}} \mathrm{sg}[c_v]\,\log p_v
\mathcal{L}_{\#4833} = \htmlClass{t2}{\underbrace{-A\,\mathrm{sg}[w]\,\log p_y}_{\text{REINFORCE only}}} + A\sum_{v\in\text{K}} \mathrm{sg}[c_v]\,\log p_v

With disable_ppo_ratio: true and token-level loss, #4818's first term is that same REINFORCE term, so #4818 already covers #4833's loss. One small difference: #4833 computes w from the current log-probs, while #4818 uses the standard importance weight from the old ones. These match on the first update of a batch.

What to take from #4833 after #4818 lands — proposed, not in either PR

So what. If you accept #4818, you get the same correction, but only for the v2 trainer with unfiltered sampling. #4833's two real additions are top-k/top-p through vLLM's sampling mask and Automodel TP/CP. Both port cleanly onto #4818's columns, and the first is already the planned follow-up. Its other addition, v1 and Gym-without-capture support, is worth taking only if v1 users need score centering. Do not merge #4833 as is: it would add a second, incompatible copy of the feature (score_centering_top_k next to score_centering), and it pins a personal Gym fork.
