Which one is the legacy path?

in_order — and it is not a close call
Legacy ReplayBuffer.sample() does three things in order: evict outside the age window, filter to that window, then narrow to target_weight_versions[i] == current_weight_version and return None unless a full num_prompt_groups are intended for this step — "so no trajectory loses its last chance to be used for its intended training step." That is exact target-step matching with all-or-nothing stalling, i.e. in_order. The age window is only a secondary guard. legacy replay_buffer.py L250-271
“Isn’t there a FIFO where everything gets used and nothing is evicted?”
Yes — that is in_order with min_groups_for_streaming_train = num_prompts_per_step. _finalize_selection returns None below min_prompt_groups, so the trainer stalls at step N until the whole batch stamped N is ready, then consumes all of it. Its evict can only fire for a group that commits after its step already passed — a straggler, not routine waste. The sync recipe sets exactly this. So the mode you were reaching for already exists; the guide just never says that in_order is the zero-waste option and windowed is the deliberately-lossy one.
