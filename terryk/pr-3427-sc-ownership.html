Three “why” questions the prose leaves open

Why does the driver build everything instead of the actor?
SingleControllerActorArgs is a dataclass of fully-constructed objects that the driver cloudpickles in, so the actor's __init__ does no construction work. Per the guide, running setup inside a nested Ray actor breaks runtime_env resolution. setup.py L68
…then why is Logger the one exception?
Because it can't be cloudpickled — wandb/tensorboard backends hold a _thread.lock. It is the only heavy object constructed inside the actor. single_controller.py L124
Why rebind _rollout_manager._tq_buffer?
rollout_manager and tq_buffer are separate fields of the args dataclass, so Ray deserializes them as two different buffer objects. Without the rebind the writer and the sampler would each hold their own copy and the sampler would never see committed groups. single_controller.py L120-122
